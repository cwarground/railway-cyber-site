EN 50159: Safeguarding Rail Communications

Railway Cyber Intel · 30 Jul 2026

EN 50159, titled 'Safety-related communication in transmission systems', is a crucial standard for the railway industry. Published by the European Committee for Electrotechnical Standardization (CENELEC), it specifies requirements to ensure safe operation of communication systems used on railways. These include data networks, telecommunication systems, and signaling equipment that facilitate train-to-ground communications.

Scope & Requirements

The standard applies to both rolling stock and fixed installations within the railway system. It mandates that communication systems must be designed, implemented, and maintained in a way that ensures their safety integrity is not compromised under any circumstances. This includes protection against various threats such as interference, unauthorized access, or malfunction.

Key Clauses

  • 4.1 Purpose: Defines the scope of the standard to cover all communication systems impacting railway safety.
  • 5.2.1 Communication Integrity: Emphasizes the need for continuous monitoring and integrity checking of communication channels.
  • 5.3.1 Resilience Against Attacks: Mandates resilience against various types of attacks, including hacking attempts or other unauthorized accesses.

Implementation & Compliance

To comply with EN 50159, railway operators and infrastructure managers must perform a thorough risk analysis of their communication systems. This analysis should identify potential vulnerabilities and threats that could compromise the safety integrity of these systems. Based on this analysis, specific safeguards and security measures should be implemented to mitigate identified risks.

Practical Implication

For rail operators and cybersecurity engineers, adhering to EN 50159 means implementing comprehensive security measures in their communication networks. This includes regular vulnerability assessments, intrusion detection systems, secure encryption of data transmissions, and staff training on cybersecurity best practices. By doing so, they can safeguard the safety-critical communications that keep railways running securely.
