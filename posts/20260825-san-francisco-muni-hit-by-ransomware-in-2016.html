Attack Case Study

San Francisco Muni Hit by Ransomware in 2016

Railway Cyber Intel · 25 Aug 2026
Photo by Sora Shimazaki on Pexels

In September 2016, the Municipal Railway of San Francisco (Muni) fell victim to a ransomware attack that disrupted services and raised concerns about the cybersecurity of public transportation systems. The attackers gained access to Muni's network, encrypted data on critical systems, and demanded payment to restore functionality.

Attack Details

The ransomware attack caused significant disruption to Muni's operations, forcing the temporary shutdown of some ticketing systems and hindering the ability to manage bus and train schedules. The incident was attributed to a phishing email that allowed the attackers to infiltrate the transit authority's network.

Impact on Rail Operations

The attack highlighted the vulnerability of rail networks to cyber threats and underscored the need for robust cybersecurity measures. Muni faced public criticism and financial losses due to the outage, emphasizing the potential consequences of such incidents on public trust and operational efficiency.

Response and Recovery

In response to the incident, Muni collaborated with law enforcement and cybersecurity experts to investigate the attack and fortify its defenses against future threats. The transit authority also developed a recovery plan to minimize the impact of similar incidents in the future.

Practical Implication

Rail operators must prioritize cybersecurity to protect critical infrastructure from cyber attacks. Implementing industry-standard security protocols, conducting regular risk assessments, and developing incident response plans are essential steps toward safeguarding rail networks from potential threats.
