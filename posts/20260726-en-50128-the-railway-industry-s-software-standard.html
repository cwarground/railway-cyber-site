EN 50128: The Railway Industry's Software Standard

Railway Cyber Intel · 26 Jul 2026

EN 50128 is a crucial standard within the railway industry, focusing on the development of software used in control and protection systems. This international standard sets out the requirements for ensuring that software is reliable, safe, and meets the specific needs of rail operations.

Scope and Application

EN 50128 covers a wide range of software used in railway applications, from signaling systems to train management software. It applies to both new developments and modifications of existing software, ensuring consistency and quality across the industry.

Key Requirements

The standard is structured around key requirements that organizations must adhere to. These include:

  • Management Responsibility (Clause 7): This clause emphasizes the importance of senior management in promoting a safety culture and ensuring that software development meets the required standards.
  • Software Development: EN 50128 provides detailed guidelines on the software development life cycle, from initial requirements gathering to testing and validation. It also covers the use of tools and techniques for managing changes to the software during its lifecycle.
  • Risk Management (Clause 6): The standard requires organizations to identify, assess, and manage risks associated with their software development processes and the software itself.
  • Configuration Management: This aspect of the standard focuses on ensuring that all changes to the software are properly controlled, tracked, and documented.

Compliance and Certification

To ensure compliance with EN 50128, railway organizations must undergo a thorough audit process. The standard is aligned with other relevant standards, such as ISO 9001 for quality management systems and ISO/IEC 12207 for software lifecycle processes.

Practical Implication

For rail operators and security engineers, EN 50128 provides a solid framework for developing and maintaining software that is fit for purpose. By adhering to the standard's requirements, organizations can ensure that their control and protection systems are reliable, safe, and secure.

Implementing EN 50128 also helps rail operators demonstrate compliance with regulatory requirements and industry best practices. This can lead to improved safety performance, reduced risks of system failures, and increased confidence among stakeholders, including passengers and regulators.
