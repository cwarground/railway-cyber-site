TS 50701: Rail's Gold Standard for Risk Assessment

Railway Cyber Intel · 28 Jul 2026

The railway industry's OT/IT landscape is growing rapidly in complexity, with more connected systems than ever before. This presents a tantalizing target for cybercriminals. To stay ahead of threats, rail operators must rigorously assess and mitigate the cybersecurity risks to their vital infrastructure and assets.

Standard TS 50701: The Foundation

A key pillar of this effort is the Technical Standard TS 50701, published by the UK's Rail Safety and Standards Board (RSSB). This document provides a comprehensive methodology for rail operators to conduct thorough cybersecurity risk assessments of their assets.

Methodology Components

The standard breaks down its risk assessment process into several key components:

  • A common terminology and framework to categorize and prioritize risks
  • An approach for identifying both current and future risks to rail assets
  • Templates for documenting assessments, which can be tailored by each operator

Practical Implications

Implementing the TS 50701 risk assessment methodology allows rail operators to proactively identify and neutralize potential cybersecurity threats before they escalate into major incidents. This disciplined approach helps safeguard critical infrastructure and maintain passenger confidence.
