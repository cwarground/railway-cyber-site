DDoS Attack Halted Trains in Denmark

Railway Cyber Intel · 30 Jul 2026

In October 2022, the Danish State Railways (DSB) experienced a major cybersecurity incident that halted train services across the country. The attacker launched a distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attack, overwhelming DSB's IT systems and infrastructure with an onslaught of internet traffic.

Attack Details

The DDoS attack targeted DSB's ticketing systems, reservation systems, and other critical IT assets. By flooding the networks and servers with a massive volume of malicious traffic, the attackers effectively rendered these systems inoperable. This disruption had a cascading effect on train operations, as staff were unable to access key systems for scheduling, dispatching, and managing the fleet.

Impact

The attack resulted in significant delays and cancellations across the DSB network. Commuters and passengers experienced long wait times, route changes, and other disruptions throughout the day. The incident highlighted the potential consequences of cyber threats on rail operations, emphasizing the importance of robust cybersecurity measures to prevent and mitigate such incidents.

Standards and Best Practices

In light of this attack, it is crucial for rail operators to adhere to established cybersecurity standards and best practices. The International Organization for Standardization (ISO) has developed a set of guidelines specifically tailored for the railway industry, known as ISO/SAE 26262. This standard addresses the functional safety of electrical/electronic systems in vehicles, including cybersecurity aspects.

Practical Implications

Rail operators must prioritize the implementation of strong cybersecurity measures to safeguard against DDoS attacks and other cyber threats. This includes investing in robust IT infrastructure, regular security audits, employee training, and incident response planning. By adopting a proactive approach to cybersecurity, rail companies can minimize the impact of potential incidents and ensure the smooth operation of train services.
