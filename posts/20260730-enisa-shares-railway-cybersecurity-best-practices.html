ENISA Shares Railway Cybersecurity Best Practices

Railway Cyber Intel · 30 Jul 2026

The European Union Agency for Cybersecurity (ENISA) has published a report titled 'Railway Cyber Security Good Practices.' This document aims to provide a comprehensive overview of the key cybersecurity measures that should be implemented by railway operators and infrastructure managers to safeguard their networks against cyber threats.

Scope and Objectives

The good practices outlined in the ENISA report are divided into three main categories: organizational, technical, and process-related measures. The document emphasizes the importance of adopting a risk-based approach to cybersecurity and encourages railway operators to regularly assess and update their security policies and procedures.

Organizational Measures

The report highlights the significance of establishing a strong cybersecurity culture within the organization. This includes regular training for employees, as well as the appointment of dedicated cybersecurity personnel responsible for implementing and maintaining security measures. Additionally, ENISA stresses the importance of developing and maintaining an incident response plan to effectively handle any potential cyber attacks.

Technical Measures

In terms of technical measures, the report emphasizes the need for robust access control and identity management systems. This includes the use of strong authentication methods, such as two-factor authentication, and the implementation of least privilege principles to minimize the risk of unauthorized access. The document also highlights the importance of regular system updates and patches to address known vulnerabilities.

Process-related Measures

The ENISA report further discusses process-related measures, such as the implementation of a formal cybersecurity management system (CSMS) compliant with ISO/IEC 27001 standards. This includes the development of comprehensive security policies and procedures, as well as regular audits and assessments to ensure ongoing compliance with these processes.

Practical Implication

For rail operators and security engineers, the practical implications of implementing these good practices are significant. By adopting a risk-based approach to cybersecurity and following the guidelines provided by ENISA, railway companies can significantly reduce their exposure to cyber threats. This includes the protection of critical infrastructure, such as trackside systems and communication networks, from potential attacks.
