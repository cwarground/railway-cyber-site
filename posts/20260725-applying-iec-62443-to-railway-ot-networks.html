Applying IEC 62443 to Railway OT Networks

Railway Cyber Intel · 25 Jul 2026

The railway industry relies heavily on operational technology (OT) networks to run trains, maintain infrastructure, and ensure safety. As these systems become increasingly connected to information technology (IT), they face new cyber threats that could disrupt service or endanger lives if not properly secured.

IEC 62443 Overview

IEC 62443 is a family of international standards that provides guidelines for securing industrial control systems, including those used in the railway sector. It covers topics such as risk management, system categorization, and security controls at various levels of criticality.

Applying IEC 62443 to Railways

To apply IEC 62443 effectively, rail operators should start by assessing their OT networks' current security posture. This involves identifying assets, understanding the threat landscape, and prioritizing risks based on potential impact.

  • System categorization: Classify railway systems into different categories (e.g., high, medium, low) based on their potential impact if compromised. Tailor security requirements to each category.
  • Risk assessment: Conduct thorough risk assessments to identify vulnerabilities and threats specific to your rail network. Use this information to inform your cybersecurity strategy.
  • Security controls: Implement a suite of security controls across your OT environment, such as access control, encryption, and incident response plans. Ensure these controls are appropriate for the system category they're applied to.

Real-World Example: Train Traffic Management System

In 2021, a major European railway operator fell victim to a cyber attack on their train traffic management system. The attackers gained access through compromised credentials and were able to disrupt services across multiple lines. Had the operator implemented IEC 62443 guidelines rigorously, they might have prevented or mitigated this incident.

Practical Implications

For rail operators looking to enhance their OT cybersecurity posture, applying IEC 62443 offers a structured and risk-based approach. By categorizing systems, assessing risks, and implementing appropriate security controls, you can create a more resilient railway network against cyber threats.
