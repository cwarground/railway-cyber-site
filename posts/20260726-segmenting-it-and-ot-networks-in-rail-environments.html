Segmenting IT and OT Networks in Rail Environments

Railway Cyber Intel · 26 Jul 2026

Network Segmentation: A Crucial Component of Rail Cybersecurity

In the evolving landscape of railway operations, ensuring the security of both operational technology (OT) and information technology (IT) networks is paramount. One of the most effective ways to achieve this is through network segmentation.

Understanding Network Segmentation

Network segmentation involves dividing a network into smaller, isolated segments, each with its own specific purpose and level of access control. This approach helps prevent unauthorized access and limit the potential impact of a cyber attack by containing it to a single segment.

The Role of Standards in Network Segmentation

Various standards have been developed to guide rail operators in implementing effective network segmentation. One such standard is IEC 62443, which provides guidelines for managing cybersecurity risks throughout the life cycle of an industrial control system. Specifically, it emphasizes the importance of segmenting networks and defining clear access policies for each segment.

Real-World Attacks and the Importance of Segmentation

In recent years, we have witnessed several high-profile cyber attacks targeting the rail industry. These incidents have highlighted the critical need for robust cybersecurity measures, including network segmentation. For example, in 2019, hackers gained unauthorized access to a major European railway's IT systems, disrupting services and causing significant disruption.

Best Practices for Implementing Network Segmentation

To effectively implement network segmentation in rail environments, operators should consider the following best practices:

  • Conduct thorough risk assessments to identify critical assets and potential vulnerabilities.
  • Implement access controls and monitoring on each segment to detect and respond to threats promptly.
  • Regularly review and update segmentation policies to adapt to changing risks and requirements.

Practical Implication

For rail operators, investing in network segmentation can provide a powerful layer of protection against cyber threats. By isolating sensitive OT systems from the wider IT network, operators can ensure that any breach is contained and minimized, reducing the risk of widespread disruption and damage.
